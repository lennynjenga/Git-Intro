##RECIPE FOR COOKING OMENA

Ingredients
-Omena 500g
-Cooking Oil 2 tqblespoons
onions 1 large,chopped
Tomatoes 2 medium, chopped

procedure
-Boil the omena for 15 minutes 
-wash the omena and place in a separate plate
-put oil in a sufuria
-Deep fry the omena till golden brown
-add onions and fry till golden browm
-add tomatoes and fry for 5 minutes
-add the fried omena and mix well
-cook for 5 minutes and serve hot with ugali or rice
